Hi, I am Wisey!

Wisey is PrepWise’s friendly owl and your personal study buddy. With her graduation cap and round glasses, she’s always ready to help you plan your week, pick the right study technique, and keep track of your deadlines. Wisey believes that studying is easier when you have a plan, and a little encouragement along the way. She and her PrepBuddies are here to help you work toward the marks you want to achieve.

About

PrepWise helps students plan, study, and succeed. Choose a study plan, try a new technique, and keep your deadlines in check, all in one place. With Wisey by your side, studying feels a little less stressful and a lot more organized.

About PrepWise

PrepWise is a study planner and resource hub made for junior high, senior high, and college students. We help you organize your semester with study plans, proven study techniques, and a calendar for your deadlines. Along the way, Wisey, your friendly study buddy, is here to guide you to the marks you want to achieve.
